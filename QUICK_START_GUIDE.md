## 💾 Working with Backup Profiles

### **Create a New Profile**
1. Go to **Backup Profiles**
2. Click **➕ New Profile**
3. Fill in:
   - **Profile Name** - e.g., "Daily Full Backup"
   - **Backup Path** - Optional custom path
   - **Backup Type** - Full, Incremental, or Differential
   - **Schedule** - Hourly, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly
   - Toggle **Activate** to enable it
4. Click **Create Profile**

### **Edit a Profile**
1. Go to **Backup Profiles**
2. Click on any profile card
3. Click **Edit Profile**
4. Make changes and click **Save Changes**

### **Delete a Profile**
1. Go to **Backup Profiles**
2. Click **Edit Profile** on the profile
3. Click **Delete** (in the modal footer)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### **Q: How often should I backup?**
A: At minimum daily, or hourly for critical data. Use **Settings** to enable automatic backups.

### **Q: Can I have multiple backup profiles?**
A: Yes! Create profiles for different data types or schedules in **Backup Profiles**.

### **Q: Is my data encrypted?**
A: By default, backups are secure. Enable additional encryption in **Settings** → **Security** for extra protection.

### **Q: How do I recover deleted data?**
A: Go to **Restore / Export**, select a backup before deletion, and click **Restore**.

### **Q: Can I move backups to cloud storage?**
A: Yes! Connect Google Drive in **Google Drive Sync** and enable auto-sync.

### **Q: What if backup fails?**
A: Check **Backup History** for error details. See the error badges and click them for more info.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### **Backup Not Starting**
1. Ensure you have an active profile
2. Check that Tally is connected (🟢 indicator on Dashboard)
3. Verify sufficient disk space
4. Check **Backup History** for previous errors

### **Tally Not Connecting**
1. Ensure Tally software is running
2. Check network connection
3. Restart the backup app
4. Review logs in Settings (Log Level: Debug)

### **Google Drive Not Syncing**
1. Go to **Google Drive Sync**
2. Disconnect and reconnect
3. Check your Google account permissions
4. Try **Sync Now** button

### **High Backup Times**
1. Large dataset? Incremental backups are faster
2. Check disk speed and health
3. Reduce other app load during backup
4. Consider splitting into smaller profiles
