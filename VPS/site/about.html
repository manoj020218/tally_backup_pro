About TallyBackup Pro

TallyBackup Pro is built for Tally users who need production-grade data protection without complicated server setup. The goal is simple: keep accounting operations safe, auditable, and recoverable.

Our Mission

Empower Indian SMB accounting teams to run reliable backup operations with confidence. We focus on Tally-aware workflows, transparent controls, and practical automation.

What We Build

Key product capabilities include:

  • Selective voucher and master-data backup from Tally
  • Incremental date-range logic
  • Local backup + Google Drive sync
  • Restore visibility and export-ready backup history
  • License-managed commercial desktop distribution
