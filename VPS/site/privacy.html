Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 23, 2026

1. Overview

TallyBackup Pro is a desktop software product for backup and sync of Tally-related business data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection.

2. Data We Process

  • Locally processed Tally export data selected by the user.
  • Backup metadata such as run history, timestamps, and file paths on local machine.
  • License and activation metadata needed for product validation.
  • Google OAuth tokens only when user enables Google Drive sync.

3. Google OAuth and Drive Access

If you connect Google Drive, TallyBackup Pro uses OAuth 2.0 authorization to access your Drive account with the minimum scopes configured by the app. We do not ask for your Google password.

4. Storage and Security

  • Data backups are stored on your chosen local path and optionally your Google Drive.
  • OAuth token payloads are encrypted at rest in local app storage.
  • You control retention settings and can disconnect Google Drive at any time.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your Tally backup contents with third parties for advertising. License validation traffic is used only for product licensing and security controls.

6. Your Choices

  • You may use local-only backup without enabling cloud sync.
  • You may remove Google Drive connection from the app settings.
  • You can request account/license-related support via email.
